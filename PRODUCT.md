# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: static Vite site using semantic HTML, CSS, and small progressive-enhancement
JavaScript. The choice matches the existing Git, Docker, Nginx, and VPS deployment pattern
already used for the candidate's role-specific sites.

## Users

- The Upwork client evaluating Nguyen Quoc Hung for the “Bilingual Sourcing & Procurement
  Specialist (Vietnam) – Build Our Procurement System” contract.
- Hiring stakeholders who need to judge whether operations experience can translate into a
  practical, maintainable procurement system.

## Product Purpose

Present a concise, evidence-backed project proposal. Success means the client can quickly
understand the proposed procurement system, verify why the candidate can build it, and continue
the hiring conversation through Upwork.

## Positioning

The proposal connects procurement design to downstream operating reality. It shows that the
candidate has managed the distribution-center consequences of supplier, purchasing, inventory,
delivery, capacity, and retail decisions and has separately built a private operations system
from the ground up.

## Operating Context

The page will be reviewed during a short Upwork screening session on desktop or mobile. The
client needs a quick path from role requirements to verified evidence, a concrete system plan,
and a bounded implementation approach.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One-page responsive English proposal at `sp-vn.corepathfocus.com`.
- No direct contact information or off-platform communication request before an Upwork contract.
- No claim of owning a supplier network, negotiating savings, or leading procurement unless it is
  supported by supplied evidence.
- Proposed workflow is an initial plan to be confirmed during a paid discovery phase.
- Five client application questions are answered in collapsed, accessible sections below the
  delivery plan so the page remains compact while detailed responses stay available.
- Static production output, deployed through Git, Docker, Nginx, and the existing VPS gateway.

## Brand Commitments

- Candidate: Nguyen Quoc Hung.
- Role: Bilingual Sourcing & Procurement Specialist — Vietnam.
- Voice: practical, precise, systems-minded, and implementation-led.
- Avoid inflated adjectives and unsupported claims.

## Evidence on Hand

- 12+ years across warehouse operations, distribution centers, fulfillment, logistics analytics,
  WMS/ERP, SOPs, KPI governance, and process stabilization.
- Managed an approximately 60,000 m² ambient and cold-chain 3PL operation serving Central Retail
  Vietnam, with 50+ staff, up to 18,000 pallets/day, and approximately 800 tons/day.
- The candidate's operating base is the Long An industrial corridor in Vietnam's Southern Key
  Economic Zone. Official investment-promotion data reports 26 industrial zones eligible to
  receive investment and 997 foreign-invested projects in those zones; Taiwan and China are among
  the investor origins with high project counts. The candidate has managed local staff familiar
  with the area's people, relationship practices, and Chinese-invested working environments.
- Managed a 10,000 m² Boxme fulfillment operation and helped recover a WMS-related backlog of more
  than 2,000 returned orders.
- Lazada experience includes BI/data work and supervision of a roughly 7,000 m² sorting center at
  approximately 300,000 parcels/day.
- Private `Work_app` repository began in June 2024 and grew into a Django/PostgreSQL warehouse
  operations workspace covering capacity, loading, workforce, vehicles, pricing, KPI, forecast,
  finance, access control, exports, and production deployment.
- No client testimonial, procurement savings figure, or direct sourcing portfolio was supplied.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the system the client needs, then connect each part to operating evidence.
2. Demonstrate implementation capability rather than relying on seniority language.
3. Separate verified experience from the proposed delivery plan.
4. Keep the page useful without animation and safe to share before an Upwork contract.
5. Make the next step a paid discovery conversation on Upwork.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic landmarks, keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, responsive text,
44px touch targets, and reduced-motion support are required.
